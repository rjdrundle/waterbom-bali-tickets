The world's most-loved water park

Waterbom Bali opened in 1993 and has spent three decades turning a single day into the highlight of the whole trip. Spread across 3.8 hectares of lush tropical gardens in Kuta, it packs 22+ world-class water slides, a 250-metre lazy river, a FlowRider surf simulator, kids' zones and some of the best food and lounging on the island.

It's not just big — it's the best. Waterbom Bali is consistently ranked the #1 water park in Asia and one of the top water parks in the world by TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice, with a reputation for spotless grounds, genuinely thrilling rides and world-class safety and lifeguard standards.

The park is also a sustainability leader — a certified green attraction that has preserved much of its original tree canopy, so you slide through actual jungle rather than concrete. It runs entirely cashless on the tap-to-pay Splash Band, so once you're in, your phone and wallet can stay dry in the locker.

What is Kuta?

Kuta is Bali's most famous beach town, on the island's south-west coast just minutes from Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS). For decades it's been the tourist heart of Bali — the place travellers land first and where the island's holiday energy is loudest.

Kuta is best known for its long golden-sand surf beach (a legendary spot for learning to surf), spectacular west-coast sunsets, buzzing nightlife, the Beachwalk shopping mall, and endless cafés, warungs and street shopping. It flows straight into neighbouring Legian and Seminyak, so it's an easy base for the whole south of Bali.

Waterbom Bali sits right in Kuta on Jl. Kartika Plaza — so wherever you're staying in Kuta, Legian or Seminyak, the park is a short ride away. Come for the slides, stay for the sunset on the beach.
