What is Kuta?

Kuta is Bali's most famous beach town, on the island's south-west coast just minutes from Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS). It's known for its long golden-sand surf beach, legendary sunsets, buzzing nightlife, and the Beachwalk shopping mall — the tourist heart of Bali. Waterbom Bali sits right in Kuta on Jl. Kartika Plaza, so wherever you stay in Kuta, Legian or Seminyak, the park is a short ride away.
