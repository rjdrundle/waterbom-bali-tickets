Best time to visit

Arrive at 9 AM opening on a weekday for the shortest queues and the coolest slides before the midday sun.

Quietest months are Feb–Mar and Oct–Nov. Busiest are Jul–Aug and the December holidays — book ahead and go early.

What to bring

  • Swimwear & a change of clothes
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Towel (or rent one inside)
  • Waterproof phone pouch
  • Your QR e-voucher + ID for the Splash Band deposit
