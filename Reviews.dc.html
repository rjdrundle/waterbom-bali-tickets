Our honest verdict

Waterbom Bali earns its ranking. The grounds are genuinely spotless, the landscaping turns a water park into a jungle, and the headline slides — The Climax, The Constrictor, Smashdown 2.0 — are world-class thrills you won't find elsewhere in Bali.

The one thing to know: in-park extras (lockers, towels, food, some premium activities) add up, since it's cashless on the Splash Band. Budget for it, bring your own towel, and it's smooth sailing.

For a full day of fun for couples, families and thrill-seekers alike, it's the best water park in Asia — and worth booking online to save and skip the queue.
